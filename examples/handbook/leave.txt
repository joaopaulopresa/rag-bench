# Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees are entitled to twenty-six days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to five unused days may be carried over and must be taken by the end of March of the following year.

## Sick leave

An employee who is ill must inform their manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required from the fourth consecutive day of illness.

## Parental leave

Each parent may take up to sixteen weeks of parental leave at full pay. Parental leave must be requested in writing at least eight weeks before it starts.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to three months may be granted after two years of service. Requests are decided by the department head together with the human resources team.
